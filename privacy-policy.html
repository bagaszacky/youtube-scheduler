Privacy Policy

YouTube Scheduler Tool

Version 2.8.1 Beta

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how YouTube Scheduler Tool handles information when users use the application.

YouTube Scheduler Tool is a desktop application developed by Bagaszzz.

2. Google Account and YouTube Access

YouTube Scheduler Tool uses Google's OAuth authentication system to allow users to connect their Google and YouTube accounts.

The application requests only the permissions necessary for its YouTube scheduling and account management features.

These permissions may include access to upload YouTube videos and read YouTube account information.

3. Information We Access

Depending on the features used, the application may access:

  • Google account identification information
  • Email address associated with the Google account
  • YouTube channel information
  • YouTube video information required by the application
  • Video files selected by the user for upload
  • Thumbnail files selected by the user
  • Schedule information created by the user

4. Local Storage

Authentication information, account information, schedules, upload progress, and application logs may be stored locally on the user's computer.

The application does not intentionally upload this local application data to a separate server operated by the developer.

5. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by the application is used to provide the application's YouTube scheduling and upload features.

The information is not sold or rented to third parties.

6. Third-Party Services

YouTube Scheduler Tool uses Google APIs and Google's OAuth authentication services.

Use of Google services is also subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Deletion

Users can remove locally stored application data by deleting the relevant account and application data from the application folders on their computer.

Users can also revoke the application's access to their Google account through their Google Account security settings.

8. Data Security

Users are responsible for protecting their computer and local application files from unauthorized access.

Users should not share authentication credentials or account data stored by the application.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data handling practices change.
