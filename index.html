YouTube Scheduler Tool

Desktop YouTube Upload Scheduling Application

Version 2.8.1 Beta

About

YouTube Scheduler Tool is a desktop application designed to help YouTube creators organize and manage scheduled video uploads.

The application connects to a user's YouTube account through Google's official OAuth authentication system.

Features

  • Schedule YouTube video uploads
  • Upload multiple videos
  • Add video thumbnails
  • Manage video titles and descriptions
  • Cancel and resume upload processes
  • Retry failed uploads
  • View upload progress and status
  • Manage multiple YouTube accounts locally

How It Works

Users sign in with their Google account and authorize the application to access the YouTube features required for uploading and managing scheduled videos.

Account authentication data and application schedule data are stored locally on the user's computer.

Privacy
